Our Story

Built by Hand,
Meant to Last.

The Beginning

A workshop, a wager,
and a lot of sawdust.

WOODORA started in 2011 in a single rented workshop in Jaipur, with three carpenters who believed furniture should be bought once, not replaced every few years. What began as custom commissions for neighbours grew into a house that now ships nationwide — without ever giving up the workshop floor.

Our Craft

Every joint,
cut by hand.

No particleboard, no shortcuts. We kiln-dry our own timber, cut every joint on site, and finish each piece with oils that deepen with age instead of wearing thin.
